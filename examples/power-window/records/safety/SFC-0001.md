## Argument

Demonstration data: written for the worked example, not by a person who stands behind it.

The window control software meets the safety requirements the customer allocated to it at ASIL B. The software safety requirements carry that ASIL down to the units (no ASIL finding of the trace check); every ASIL B component is analysed (SAN-0001) and WindowCmd is free from interference with them (SAN-0002); the units are verified with the methods and coverage of PLN-0001, the integration and qualification with those of PLN-0004 and PLN-0005; the tools are evaluated (TEV-0001, TEV-0002); the plan and the analyses passed their confirmation reviews. Left to the customer: testing on the target (clause 6:11), the timing measurement the analyses assume, everything at system and vehicle level.
