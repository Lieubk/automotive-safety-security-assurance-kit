Criteria 1 to 4 of SWR-0008. The movement command is read at its measurement point
(PLN-0005), so that it is not confused with the end of a stalled movement by MotorDrive.

## Preconditions

Test program `tests/qualification/test_qualification.c`, built by
`tests/qualification/run_tests.py` from the software of the last integration step
(PLN-0004, step application) and the platform double; stimuli and measurement points as
PLN-0005 names them. A cycle is 5 ms; every fourth cycle the 20 ms task follows.
"Referenced": start, switch request OPEN for 300 ms with the window at the open end
stop (no pulses), then NONE for 40 ms. "A movement by n pulses": the switch starts the
drive, n pulses follow 3 ms apart from 3 ms after the drive started, and the switch is
released so that the drive stops in the cycle of the last pulse. "Pulses at v mm/s":
a pulse train with the period that 0.5 mm takes at v mm/s. Positions in counts of 0.5 mm.

## Steps

1. Referenced; a closing movement by 360 pulses; AUTO_CLOSE for 60 ms, then NONE; pulses at 150 mm/s until the position is 0, then none.
2. Referenced; AUTO_CLOSE for 60 ms, then NONE, pulses at 150 mm/s; after 0.5 s OPEN.
3. Referenced; AUTO_CLOSE for 60 ms, then NONE, pulses at 150 mm/s for 1 s; a drop until the request is set; then pulses at 150 mm/s until the request is cleared and 200 ms more.
4. Start without reference; AUTO_CLOSE for 60 ms, then NONE; 200 ms.

## Expected result

1. Position 600 (300.0 mm) before; command closing within 40 ms of AUTO_CLOSE and in every cycle until 195 ms after the speed became 0; stopped 220 ms after.
2. Within 40 ms of OPEN the command is no longer closing.
3. Command stopped 40 ms after the request was set, and in every cycle after that.
4. Command and bridge stopped in every cycle.
