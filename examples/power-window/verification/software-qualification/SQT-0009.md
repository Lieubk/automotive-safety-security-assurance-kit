Criteria 1 to 3 of SWR-0009. The movement command is read at its measurement point (PLN-0005).

## Preconditions

Test program `tests/qualification/test_qualification.c`, built by
`tests/qualification/run_tests.py` from the software of the last integration step
(PLN-0004, step application) and the platform double; stimuli and measurement points as
PLN-0005 names them. A cycle is 5 ms; every fourth cycle the 20 ms task follows.
"Referenced": start, switch request OPEN for 300 ms with the window at the open end
stop (no pulses), then NONE for 40 ms. "A movement by n pulses": the switch starts the
drive, n pulses follow 3 ms apart from 3 ms after the drive started, and the switch is
released so that the drive stops in the cycle of the last pulse. "Pulses at v mm/s":
a pulse train with the period that 0.5 mm takes at v mm/s. Positions in counts of 0.5 mm.
Pulses at 150 mm/s throughout.

## Steps

1. Referenced; CLOSE for 500 ms, then NONE for 200 ms.
2. OPEN for 500 ms, then NONE for 200 ms.
3. For each of the values 4, 5, 6 and 7: OPEN for 100 ms, then the value.
4. OPEN for 200 ms; then the frames stop: the stack keeps OPEN for 100 ms and then delivers 7.

## Expected result

1. Command closing from at most 40 ms after the start of the request in every cycle until its end; stopped at most 40 ms after its end and in every cycle after that.
2. As 1, with opening.
3. Command opening, then stopped within 40 ms of the value.
4. Command opening; stopped within 40 ms of the 7, at most 140 ms after the last frame.
