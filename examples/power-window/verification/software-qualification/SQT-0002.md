Criteria 1 to 4 of SWR-0002. Criterion 3 asks for a stop of 5 ms between two opening
phases; the switch cannot stop the drive for less than a cycle of the command logic, so
the stop is a fault of the bridge driver for one cycle (IFC-0008). MotorDrive ends a
commanded movement after 250 ms at 0 mm/s (CMP-0005), so the second phase is judged 10 ms
after the stop - where an uninterrupted count would already have referenced - and again
200 ms after it.

## Preconditions

Test program `tests/qualification/test_qualification.c`, built by
`tests/qualification/run_tests.py` from the software of the last integration step
(PLN-0004, step application) and the platform double; stimuli and measurement points as
PLN-0005 names them. A cycle is 5 ms; every fourth cycle the 20 ms task follows.
"Referenced": start, switch request OPEN for 300 ms with the window at the open end
stop (no pulses), then NONE for 40 ms. "A movement by n pulses": the switch starts the
drive, n pulses follow 3 ms apart from 3 ms after the drive started, and the switch is
released so that the drive stops in the cycle of the last pulse. "Pulses at v mm/s":
a pulse train with the period that 0.5 mm takes at v mm/s. Positions in counts of 0.5 mm.

## Steps

1. Start; CLOSE with pulses at 150 mm/s for 175 ms; NONE for 40 ms; no pulses for 300 ms.
2. OPEN with the window at the open end stop; 195 ms after the drive started opening, and 200 ms after.
3. Start; OPEN at the open end stop; after 195 ms of opening, a bridge fault for one cycle (5 ms); then 10 ms and 200 ms after the stop.
4. Referenced; a closing movement by 100 pulses; 60 ms without pulses; OPEN without pulses; 195 ms and 200 ms after the drive started opening.

## Expected result

1. `WindowPos_GetPosition` returns E_NOT_OK and 65535: not available.
2. Not available at 195 ms; 960 (480.0 mm) at 200 ms.
3. Not available at both times.
4. Position 860 (430.0 mm), speed 0 before opening; 860 at 195 ms; 960 at 200 ms.
