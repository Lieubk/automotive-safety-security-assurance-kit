Criteria 1 to 5 of SWR-0004. In the integrated software the window opening and the speed
come from the same pulses, so the opening cannot be held at 5.5 mm while the speed
drops: in 10 ms at 129 mm/s it falls by 1.3 mm. Criterion 4 is therefore shown here at
5.0 mm (no request) and 8.0 mm (request); the boundary between 5.0 mm and 5.5 mm with
the opening held is shown at unit level by UT-0016, as PLN-0005 states.

## Preconditions

Test program `tests/qualification/test_qualification.c`, built by
`tests/qualification/run_tests.py` from the software of the last integration step
(PLN-0004, step application) and the platform double; stimuli and measurement points as
PLN-0005 names them. A cycle is 5 ms; every fourth cycle the 20 ms task follows.
"Referenced": start, switch request OPEN for 300 ms with the window at the open end
stop (no pulses), then NONE for 40 ms. "A movement by n pulses": the switch starts the
drive, n pulses follow 3 ms apart from 3 ms after the drive started, and the switch is
released so that the drive stops in the cycle of the last pulse. "Pulses at v mm/s":
a pulse train with the period that 0.5 mm takes at v mm/s. Positions in counts of 0.5 mm.

## Steps

1. Referenced; CLOSE with pulses at 150 mm/s until the position is 600 (300.0 mm) or less; then pulses at 129 mm/s.
2. As step 1, but 130 mm/s for 1 s.
3. As step 1, but the speed falls by 0.5 mm/s every cycle (100 mm/s per second) to 100 mm/s, held for 0.5 s.
4. As step 1 at a position of 10 (5.0 mm) or less; again at 16 (8.0 mm) or less; and after a start without reference.
5. Referenced; CLOSE; pulses at 29 mm/s from the start of the drive; then again with 30 mm/s for 1 s.

## Expected result

1. Reversal request set within 2 cycles (10 ms).
2. Request clear throughout.
3. Request clear throughout.
4. No request within 10 cycles at 5.0 mm; request within 2 cycles at 8.0 mm and without reference.
5. Request set no later than 22 cycles (110 ms) after the drive started closing; at 30 mm/s clear throughout.
