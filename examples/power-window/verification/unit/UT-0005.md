Normal case and error case of `HallCapture_GetSnapshot`. Design basis: UNT-0001, function
contract and Error handling.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit HallCapture (`tests/unit/test_HallCapture.c` with
`src/windowpos/HallCapture.c`). The two exclusive area services are replaced by stubs
that count their calls. "Read" means a call of `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` that returns
`E_OK`.

## Steps

1. `HallCapture_Init`, edges at 2000, 5000 and 5100. Clear the stub counters and call
   `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` with a valid pointer.
2. Clear the stub counters and call `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` with a null pointer.

## Expected result

1. `E_OK`. The exclusive area was entered once and left once, was never nested and is left
   at the end. `pulse_count` 2, `rejected_count` 1, `last_edge_us` 5000, `period_us` 3000.
2. `E_NOT_OK`. The exclusive area was neither entered nor left.
