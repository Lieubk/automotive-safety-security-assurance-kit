Boundary values 39 and 40 cycles, and every condition that restarts the count. Design
basis: UNT-0002, Behavior, step 5; SWR-0002.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit WindowPos (`tests/unit/test_WindowPos.c` with
`src/windowpos/WindowPos.c`). `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` is replaced by a stub that
returns prepared pulse data and a prepared return value. "Referenced" means 40 steps
with direction opening and no pulse; unless a period is given, the pulse data keeps the
speed at 0 mm/s.

## Steps

1. `WindowPos_Init`; 39 steps opening without pulse; read the position. One more step; read.
   300 more such steps; read.
2. `WindowPos_Init`; 39 steps opening, 1 step stopped, 39 steps opening; read. One more step
   opening; read.
3. `WindowPos_Init`; 39 steps opening; 1 step opening at 100 mm/s (2 pulses 5000 us apart);
   60 ms later 39 steps opening without pulse; read. One more step; read.
4. `WindowPos_Init`; 100 steps closing and 100 steps stopped, without pulse; read.
5. 40 steps opening; read. 1 step closing with 100 pulses; read. 40 steps opening without
   pulse; read.

## Expected result

1. `E_NOT_OK`, 65535; then `E_OK`, 960; still `E_OK`, 960 after the 300 steps (the cycle count
   stops at 40).
2. `E_NOT_OK`; then `E_OK`, 960.
3. `E_NOT_OK`; then `E_OK`, 960.
4. `E_NOT_OK`.
5. 960, 860, 960. Every step of the test returns `E_OK`.
