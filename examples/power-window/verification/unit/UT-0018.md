Boundary values 239 and 240 counts of opening travel, and the limit at 960 counts.
Design basis: UNT-0003, state REVERSING; SWR-0005, criteria 1 and 2.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit AntiPinch (`tests/unit/test_AntiPinch.c` with
`src/antipinch/AntiPinch.c`); no stubs. A step is written as (position, speed, drive
direction); positions in counts of 0.5 mm, speeds in mm/s. "Monitoring at p / v" means
`AntiPinch_Init` followed by 20 steps (p, v, closing). The status is read with
`AntiPinch_GetStatus` after every step named.

## Steps

1. Monitoring at 600 / 150; 2 steps (600, 100, closing).
2. Steps (600, 0, stopped), (700, 150, opening), (839, 150, opening).
3. 1 step (840, 150, opening).
4. Monitoring at 800 / 150; 2 steps (800, 100, closing); 1 step (959, 150, opening).
5. 1 step (960, 150, opening).
6. 20 steps (960, 150, closing).

## Expected result

1. REVERSING, request TRUE.
2. REVERSING, request TRUE after each step.
3. IDLE, request FALSE.
4. REVERSING, request TRUE.
5. IDLE, request FALSE.
6. MONITORING, request FALSE: the next closing movement is supervised as usual.
