Normal case. Design basis: UNT-0001, Behavior, step 1 of `HallCapture_OnEdge`, and the
postconditions of `HallCapture_Init`.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit HallCapture (`tests/unit/test_HallCapture.c` with
`src/windowpos/HallCapture.c`). The two exclusive area services are replaced by stubs
that count their calls. "Read" means a call of `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` that returns
`E_OK`.

## Steps

1. Call `HallCapture_Init` and read.
2. Call `HallCapture_OnEdge(1000)` and read.
3. Call `HallCapture_OnEdge(5000)`, then `HallCapture_Init`, and read.
4. Call `HallCapture_OnEdge(5100)` and read.

## Expected result

1. `pulse_count` 0, `rejected_count` 0, `period_us` 0.
2. `pulse_count` 1, `rejected_count` 0, `last_edge_us` 1000, `period_us` 0.
3. `pulse_count` 0, `last_edge_us` 0, `period_us` 0.
4. `pulse_count` 1, `rejected_count` 0, `period_us` 0: the edge is a first edge again and is
   not measured against the edge at 5000.
