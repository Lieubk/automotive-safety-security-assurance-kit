Normal case. Design basis: UNT-0002, postconditions of `WindowPos_Init` and contracts of
the two read functions.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit WindowPos (`tests/unit/test_WindowPos.c` with
`src/windowpos/WindowPos.c`). `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` is replaced by a stub that
returns prepared pulse data and a prepared return value. "Referenced" means 40 steps
with direction opening and no pulse; unless a period is given, the pulse data keeps the
speed at 0 mm/s.

## Steps

1. `WindowPos_Init`; read position and speed.
2. Reference the position; one step closing with 2 new pulses 4000 us apart, the last one
   100 us before `now_us`; read position and speed.
3. `WindowPos_Init`; read position and speed.

## Expected result

1. `WindowPos_GetPosition` returns `E_NOT_OK` and delivers 65535; `WindowPos_GetSpeed` returns
   `E_OK` and delivers 0.
2. Position `E_OK`, 958; speed `E_OK`, 125.
3. As in step 1.
