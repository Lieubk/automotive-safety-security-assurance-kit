Boundary values 30 and 29 mm/s at the lower limit of the reference speed, and the
behavior of the low-pass. Design basis: UNT-0003, reference speed; SWR-0004, criteria 3 and 5.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit AntiPinch (`tests/unit/test_AntiPinch.c` with
`src/antipinch/AntiPinch.c`); no stubs. A step is written as (position, speed, drive
direction); positions in counts of 0.5 mm, speeds in mm/s. "Monitoring at p / v" means
`AntiPinch_Init` followed by 20 steps (p, v, closing). The status is read with
`AntiPinch_GetStatus` after every step named.

## Steps

1. `AntiPinch_Init`; 20 steps (600, 30, closing).
2. 10 further steps (600, 30, closing).
3. 2 steps (600, 29, closing).
4. Monitoring at 600 / 150; 100 steps (600, v, closing) with v = 150 minus half the step
   number, rounded down: from 150 to 100 mm/s at 100 mm/s per second.
5. 200 steps (600, 100, closing).
6. 200 steps (600, 115, closing).

## Expected result

1. MONITORING, reference speed 50.
2. MONITORING, request FALSE, reference speed 50.
3. MONITORING, request FALSE after the first step; REVERSING, request TRUE after the second.
4. MONITORING, request FALSE after every step; reference speed at the end between 100 and
   110.
5. Reference speed 100.
6. MONITORING, request FALSE, reference speed 115.
