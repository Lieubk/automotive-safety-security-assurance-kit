Normal cases and boundary values 0 and 960 of the position, and the wrap of the pulse
counter. Design basis: UNT-0002, Behavior, steps 1 to 3; SWR-0001, criteria 1, 2, 3 and 5.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit WindowPos (`tests/unit/test_WindowPos.c` with
`src/windowpos/WindowPos.c`). `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` is replaced by a stub that
returns prepared pulse data and a prepared return value. "Referenced" means 40 steps
with direction opening and no pulse; unless a period is given, the pulse data keeps the
speed at 0 mm/s.

## Steps

1. Reference the position. One step closing with 100 pulses.
2. One step opening with 20 pulses.
3. One step stopped with 3 pulses.
4. One step closing with 5 pulses, one step stopped with 2 pulses.
5. Steps opening with 83, 1, 1 and 200 pulses.
6. Steps closing with 959, 1, 1 and 500 pulses.
7. Set the pulse counter of the stub to 65530 and do one step closing. Then one step opening
   with 10 pulses (the counter wraps to 4).

## Expected result

1. 860.
2. 880.
3. 883: counted in the last drive direction.
4. 876.
5. 959, 960, 960, 960.
6. 1, 0, 0, 0.
7. 0 after the first step, 10 after the second.
