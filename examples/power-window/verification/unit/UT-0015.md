Boundary values: a drop of exactly 20 mm/s, and a drop of 21 mm/s for one cycle only.
Design basis: UNT-0003, state MONITORING; SWR-0004, criterion 2.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit AntiPinch (`tests/unit/test_AntiPinch.c` with
`src/antipinch/AntiPinch.c`); no stubs. A step is written as (position, speed, drive
direction); positions in counts of 0.5 mm, speeds in mm/s. "Monitoring at p / v" means
`AntiPinch_Init` followed by 20 steps (p, v, closing). The status is read with
`AntiPinch_GetStatus` after every step named.

## Steps

1. Monitoring at 600 / 150; 40 steps (600, 130, closing).
2. Monitoring at 600 / 150; five times: 1 step (600, 129, closing), 1 step (600, 150,
   closing).
3. 2 steps (600, 129, closing).

## Expected result

1. MONITORING, request FALSE after every step.
2. MONITORING, request FALSE after every step; reference speed 150 after every pair.
3. MONITORING, request FALSE after the first step; REVERSING, request TRUE after the second.
