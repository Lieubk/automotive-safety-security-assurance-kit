Normal cases. Design basis: UNT-0002, Behavior, step 4; SWR-0003, criteria 1 and 2.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit WindowPos (`tests/unit/test_WindowPos.c` with
`src/windowpos/WindowPos.c`). `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` is replaced by a stub that
returns prepared pulse data and a prepared return value. "Referenced" means 40 steps
with direction opening and no pulse; unless a period is given, the pulse data keeps the
speed at 0 mm/s. The speed is read after every step.

## Steps

1. `WindowPos_Init`. 2 pulses with period 3333 us, the last one 500 us before `now_us`; step
   closing.
2. No new pulse; steps 5000 us and 10000 us after the last pulse.
3. One new pulse with period 12500 us, 2500 us before `now_us`.
4. One new pulse with period 3334 us.
5. One new pulse with period 5000 us, step with direction stopped. One new pulse with
   period 2500 us, step with direction opening.
6. Read the position.

## Expected result

1. 150.
2. 100, then 50.
3. 40: the longer of period and elapsed time counts.
4. 149: rounded down from 149.97.
5. 100, then 200: the speed does not depend on the drive direction.
6. `E_NOT_OK`: the speed does not need a referenced position.
