Boundary values 399 us, 400 us and 0 us of the interval check. Design basis: UNT-0001,
Behavior, step 2 of `HallCapture_OnEdge`; SWR-0001, criterion 4.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit HallCapture (`tests/unit/test_HallCapture.c` with
`src/windowpos/HallCapture.c`). The two exclusive area services are replaced by stubs
that count their calls. "Read" means a call of `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` that returns
`E_OK`.

## Steps

1. `HallCapture_Init`, edge at 1000, edge at 1399; read.
2. Edge at 1400; read.
3. Edge at 1400 again; read.

## Expected result

1. Rejected: `pulse_count` 1, `rejected_count` 1, `last_edge_us` 1000, `period_us` 0.
2. Accepted: `pulse_count` 2, `rejected_count` 1, `last_edge_us` 1400, `period_us` 400 - measured
   from the last accepted edge, not from the rejected one.
3. Rejected: `pulse_count` 2, `rejected_count` 2, `period_us` 400.
