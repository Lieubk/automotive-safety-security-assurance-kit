Normal case and boundary of the time stamp range. Design basis: UNT-0001, Behavior, step 2
of `HallCapture_OnEdge` (unsigned 32 bit subtraction).

## Preconditions

Test program of unit HallCapture (`tests/unit/test_HallCapture.c` with
`src/windowpos/HallCapture.c`). The two exclusive area services are replaced by stubs
that count their calls. "Read" means a call of `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` that returns
`E_OK`.

## Steps

1. `HallCapture_Init`, edges at 10000 and 13333; read.
2. Edge at 20000; read.
3. `HallCapture_Init`, edges at 4294967040 (0xFFFFFF00) and 3840 (0x00000F00); read.

## Expected result

1. `pulse_count` 2, `last_edge_us` 13333, `period_us` 3333.
2. `pulse_count` 3, `period_us` 6667.
3. `pulse_count` 2, `last_edge_us` 3840, `period_us` 4096 (256 us before the wrap and 3840 us
   after it).
