Boundary values of the speed calculation: 49999 us and 50000 us, 1001 us and 1000 us, the
first pulse after a standstill, and time stamps around `now_us`. Design basis: UNT-0002,
Behavior, step 4; SWR-0003, criteria 3 and 4.

## Preconditions

Test program of unit WindowPos (`tests/unit/test_WindowPos.c` with
`src/windowpos/WindowPos.c`). `HallCapture_GetSnapshot` is replaced by a stub that
returns prepared pulse data and a prepared return value. "Referenced" means 40 steps
with direction opening and no pulse; unless a period is given, the pulse data keeps the
speed at 0 mm/s. All steps with direction closing; the speed is read
after every step.

## Steps

1. `WindowPos_Init`. 2 pulses with period 3333 us; step 49999 us after the last pulse; step
   50000 us after the last pulse.
2. One new pulse that comes with a period of 3000 us; then a second new pulse with period
   4000 us.
3. New pulses with periods 49999 us, 50000 us and 5000 us, one per step.
4. New pulses with periods 1001 us, 1000 us and 400 us, one per step.
5. One new pulse with period 4000 us and a time stamp 100 us later than `now_us`.
6. One new pulse with period 3333 us and time stamp 4294963200 (0xFFFFF000); `now_us` =
   1280 (0x00000500).

## Expected result

1. 10, then 0.
2. 0 after the first pulse, whatever its period; 125 after the second.
3. 10, 0, 100: a period of 50000 us gives 0 without a new standstill.
4. 499, 500, 500.
5. 125: the time since the pulse counts as 0.
6. 93: 5376 us have passed across the wrap of the time base.
