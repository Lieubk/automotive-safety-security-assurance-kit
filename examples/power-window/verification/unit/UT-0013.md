Normal case of the transitions IDLE, SETTLING, MONITORING and back, with the boundary
values 19 and 20 closing cycles. Design basis: UNT-0003, state machine; SWR-0004 (100 ms).

## Preconditions

Test program of unit AntiPinch (`tests/unit/test_AntiPinch.c` with
`src/antipinch/AntiPinch.c`); no stubs. A step is written as (position, speed, drive
direction); positions in counts of 0.5 mm, speeds in mm/s. "Monitoring at p / v" means
`AntiPinch_Init` followed by 20 steps (p, v, closing). The status is read with
`AntiPinch_GetStatus` after every step named.

## Steps

1. `AntiPinch_Init`.
2. 5 steps (600, 150, opening) and 5 steps (600, 0, stopped).
3. 1 step (600, 0, closing).
4. 9 steps (600, 150, closing) and 9 steps (600, 40, closing): closing cycles 2 to 19.
5. 1 step (600, 150, closing): closing cycle 20.
6. 1 step (600, 150, stopped).
7. 19 steps (600, 140, closing); then 1 more.
8. 1 step (600, 140, opening).
9. `AntiPinch_Init`; 10 steps (600, 100, closing); 1 step (600, 100, opening); 19 steps
   (600, 100, closing).

## Expected result

1. IDLE, request FALSE, reference speed 0.
2. IDLE, request FALSE.
3. SETTLING, request FALSE.
4. SETTLING, request FALSE, reference speed 0: the fall from 150 to 40 causes no reaction.
5. MONITORING, request FALSE, reference speed 150.
6. IDLE, reference speed 0.
7. SETTLING after 19 steps; MONITORING with reference speed 140 after the 20th.
8. IDLE, reference speed 0.
9. IDLE after the opening step; SETTLING after the 19 steps. Every step of the test returns
   `E_OK`.
