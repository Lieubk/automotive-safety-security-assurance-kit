Interface case: edges with their time stamps through the capture interrupt (IFC-0001)
and the capture time once per cycle (IFC-0007) give the window motion WindowPos
publishes. Basis: IFC-0001 (time stamp latched at the edge; the provider rejects an
edge less than 400 us after the last accepted one), IFC-0007 (the "now" of the speed);
the values from the criteria of SWR-0001 and SWR-0003.

## Preconditions

Test program of step detection (`tests/integration/test_detection.c`), built by
`tests/integration/run_tests.py` from the units of WindowPos (CMP-0003) and AntiPinch
(CMP-0004), with the doubles of WindowCmd, MotorDrive and the platform. The MotorDrive
double publishes the motor state the test sets and records the reversal request and the
speed the 5 ms task hands to it. A cycle is one run of `OsTask_Control_5ms` by the
platform double, 5 ms after the previous one, with the capture time set to the time of
the cycle; the edges of a cycle reach the capture interrupt before the task runs.
"Referenced" means: start, motor state opening, 40 cycles without edges. Positions in
counts of 0.5 mm.

## Steps

1. Referenced.
2. Motor state closing; edges every 3000 us for 60 cycles (100 edges, the last one at the time of the last cycle).
3. An edge 399 us after the last accepted edge and another one 400 us after it; one cycle.

## Expected result

1. `WindowPos_GetPosition` returns E_OK and 960 (480.0 mm).
2. Position 860 (430.0 mm); speed 166 mm/s (0.5 mm in 3000 us, rounded down).
3. Position 859: the edge 399 us after the accepted one was rejected, the one 400 us after it was counted.
