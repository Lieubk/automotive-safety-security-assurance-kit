Interface case for the operation of IFC-0004, played by the test as its client. Basis:
IFC-0004 (direction 0 to 2, duty 30 % to 100 %, ignored for stopped; E_NOT_OK sets the
command to stopped), CMP-0005 (a movement starts at 30 %).

## Preconditions

Test program of step application (`tests/integration/test_application.c`), built by
`tests/integration/run_tests.py` from the units of WindowPos and AntiPinch and the
received sources of WindowCmd (CMP-0002) and MotorDrive (CMP-0005), with the platform
double. A cycle is one run of `OsTask_Control_5ms`; after every fourth cycle
`OsTask_Command_20ms` runs, as in VIEW-0004. The bridge command is what the platform
double received in the last call of `BridgeOutput_Set`: direction 0 off, 1 opening,
2 closing, and the duty cycle.
Only the 5 ms task runs, so that WindowCmd does not overwrite the command; edges
every 3333 us.

## Steps

1. `MotorDrive_RequestMovement(2, 100)`; 1 cycle.
2. `(3, 100)`; 1 cycle.
3. `(1, 29)`; 1 cycle; then `(1, 30)`; 1 cycle.
4. `(1, 101)`; 1 cycle.
5. `(0, 0)`; 1 cycle.

## Expected result

1. E_OK; bridge closing at 30 %.
2. E_NOT_OK; bridge off.
3. E_NOT_OK; bridge off; then E_OK; bridge opening at 30 %.
4. E_NOT_OK; bridge off.
5. E_OK; bridge off.
