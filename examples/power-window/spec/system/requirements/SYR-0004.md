## Statement

While the switch request is OPEN or CLOSE, the door module shall move the window in
the requested direction, starting and ending the movement within 100 ms after the
request has changed.

## Verification criteria

OPEN and CLOSE each applied for 1 s at a window opening of 240 mm: the motor is driven
in the requested direction from at most 100 ms after the start of the request until at
most 100 ms after its end.
