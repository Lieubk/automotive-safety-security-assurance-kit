## Statement

When the switch request AUTO_CLOSE is received, the door module shall close the window
completely without the request being held, unless a later switch request or a detected
obstacle ends the movement.

## Verification criteria

AUTO_CLOSE applied for 100 ms at window openings of 480 mm and 200 mm: the window
reaches the fully closed position. OPEN applied during the movement: the closing
movement ends within 100 ms.
