## Statement

When an obstacle has been detected while the window closes, the door module shall open
the window by 120 mm from the position of detection, or to the fully open position if
less than 120 mm of travel remain.

## Verification criteria

With the test body of DM-SYS-041 at window openings of 10 mm and 150 mm the glass comes
to rest 120 mm +/- 5 mm further open than at the moment of contact. At 400 mm it comes
to rest fully open (480 mm).
