## Responsibility

Moves the glass and delivers the signals the software needs:

- DC motor with worm gear and cable regulator; glass travel 480 mm between the fully
  closed and the fully open end stop.
- Hall sensor on the motor shaft with a ring magnet: one pulse per 0.5 mm of glass
  travel, the same in both directions (SYI-0001).
- H-bridge driver with direction inputs and pulse width modulation (SYI-0002).
- Microcontroller: 32 bit, one core, 128 KB program flash, 16 KB RAM, a free-running
  32 bit capture timer with a resolution of 1 us.

## Dynamic behavior

- Glass speed in steady movement: 80 mm/s to 200 mm/s over the supply range of 9 V to
  16 V, which is 160 to 400 Hall pulses per second.
- After the bridge has been switched on, the first Hall pulse follows within 60 ms.
- After the bridge has been switched off or reversed, the glass runs on for at most
  3 Hall pulses (1.5 mm).
- A new bridge command changes the motor voltage within 1 ms.
