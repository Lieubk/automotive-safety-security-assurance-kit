## Description

The software side of the Hall sensor pulse signal (SYI-0001). The capture interrupt of
the basic software calls the operation once for every rising edge, with the timer
value the hardware latched at the edge, not the time of the call.

Constraints on its use:

- Called from `Isr_HallCapture` only, and never nested.
- Not called before `HallCapture_Init` has run; the interrupt is enabled afterwards.
- The caller passes every edge. Filtering is done by the provider: an edge less than
  400 us after the last accepted one is counted as rejected and otherwise ignored.

Error behavior: the operation cannot fail and reports nothing to its caller. Edges
that are not delivered are lost travel; the provider cannot detect this.
