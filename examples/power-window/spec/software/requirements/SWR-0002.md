## Statement

The software shall report the window opening as not available from reset until the
drive has been driven in the opening direction at a window speed of 0 mm/s for 200 ms
without interruption, and set it to 480 mm at that moment.

## Verification criteria

1. After reset, with any sequence of closing and stopped drive: opening not available.
2. Drive opening at 0 mm/s for 195 ms: not available. After 200 ms: 480.0 mm.
3. Drive opening at 0 mm/s for 195 ms, stopped for 5 ms, opening for 195 ms: not
   available.
4. Opening available at 430.0 mm, drive opening at 0 mm/s for 200 ms: 480.0 mm.

## Analysis

The reference is taken at the open end stop only, where a wrong reference cannot shrink
the protected zone: if the window is blocked before the end stop, the reported opening
is larger than the real one, and the zone of SWR-0004 ends earlier, never later. The
closed end stop cannot be told from an obstacle without a valid opening. No
non-volatile memory is needed in release R1.0.
