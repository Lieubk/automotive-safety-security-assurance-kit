## Statement

The software shall change the window opening by 0.5 mm, within the range 0 mm to
480 mm, for every Hall sensor pulse that follows the previously counted pulse by at
least 400 us, in the direction in which the drive is driven or was driven last.

## Verification criteria

Starting from a referenced window opening of 480.0 mm, with pulses 3 ms apart:

1. 100 pulses while the drive closes: 430.0 mm.
2. 20 further pulses while the drive opens: 440.0 mm.
3. 3 further pulses after the drive has stopped: 441.5 mm.
4. A pulse 399 us after a counted pulse: opening unchanged. The same pulse 400 us after
   it: opening changed by 0.5 mm.
5. More pulses than travel remains, in either direction: the opening stays at 0.0 mm
   or at 480.0 mm.

## Analysis

One Hall sensor gives no direction. Pulses of the run-out after a change of direction
(at most 3, SYE-0002) are counted in the new direction; the error of at most 1.5 mm per
change is removed whenever the opening is referenced again (SWR-0002). Effect on the
environment: one capture interrupt per pulse, at most 400 per second in steady
movement.
