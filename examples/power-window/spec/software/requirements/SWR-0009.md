## Statement

While the switch request is OPEN or CLOSE, the software shall issue the movement
command for the requested direction, with at most 40 ms between a change of the switch
request and the change of the movement command.

## Verification criteria

1. CLOSE for 500 ms: movement command "closing" from at most 40 ms after the start of
   the request until at most 40 ms after its end, "stopped" afterwards.
2. OPEN for 500 ms: the same with "opening".
3. Switch request values 4 to 7, or no value received for 100 ms: movement command
   "stopped" within 40 ms.

## Analysis

40 ms are two cycles of the 20 ms task and lie within the 100 ms of SYR-0004. A manual
closing movement is supervised by the obstacle detection like an automatic one.
