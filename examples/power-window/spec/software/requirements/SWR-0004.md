## Statement

While the drive has been closing for at least 100 ms and the window opening is more
than 5 mm or not available, the software shall set the reversal request no later than
10 ms after the window speed has fallen more than 20 mm/s below the reference speed.

The reference speed is the window speed passed through a first-order low-pass with a
time constant of 80 ms +/- 5 ms, held while the window speed is more than 20 mm/s
below it, and never lower than 50 mm/s.

## Verification criteria

Drive closing for 1 s at 150 mm/s and a window opening of 300 mm, then:

1. Speed 129 mm/s: reversal request set within 10 ms.
2. Speed 130 mm/s for 1 s: request stays clear.
3. Speed falling by 100 mm/s per second down to 100 mm/s: request stays clear.

Further cases:

4. As case 1 at a window opening of 5.0 mm: request stays clear. At 5.5 mm and with
   the opening not available: set within 10 ms.
5. Drive closing from standstill, speed never above 29 mm/s: request set no later than
   110 ms after the start of closing. Speed 30 mm/s: request stays clear.

## Analysis

Budget: the system architecture allows 20 ms from the speed drop to the reversed motor
command (SYE-0001). 10 ms are spent here (two 5 ms cycles, so that one disturbed speed
value does not reverse the window), 5 ms in SWR-0007, 5 ms remain. Below 5 mm the
glass enters the seal and slows down by design; the limit is the one of SYR-0001.
