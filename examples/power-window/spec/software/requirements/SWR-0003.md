## Statement

Every 5 ms the software shall determine the window speed, over the range 10 mm/s to
500 mm/s with a deviation of at most 1 mm/s, as 0.5 mm divided by the longer of the
last pulse period and the time since the last pulse.

## Verification criteria

1. Pulse period 3333 us, last pulse 0.5 ms ago: 149 mm/s to 151 mm/s.
2. No further pulse: at most 100 mm/s when 5 ms have passed since the last pulse, at
   most 50 mm/s after 10 ms.
3. Pulse period or time since the last pulse of 50 ms or more: 0 mm/s, and 0 mm/s
   remains until two further pulses have arrived.
4. Pulse period of 1 ms or less: 500 mm/s.

## Analysis

The time since the last pulse lets a blocked window show as a falling speed after 5 ms,
without waiting for a pulse that may never come; SWR-0004 depends on this. Effect on
the environment: the capture time stamps and the 5 ms task need a common time base
with a resolution of 1 us (SYI-0001). One 32 bit division per cycle; no floating point.
