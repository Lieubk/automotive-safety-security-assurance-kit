## Claim

The risk of THR-0003 is carried by the customer: only software images signed by the
customer can be written into the door module.

## Rationale

The item definition (ITM-0001) states that the bootloader of the basic software accepts
only signed images and that reprogramming needs its security access. The customer
confirmed both at the TARA hand-over and takes the risk into its own analysis
(CMR-0004).
