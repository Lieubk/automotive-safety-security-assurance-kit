## Statement

The window shall move on a request received over the body network only if the request
comes from the driver's switch panel, has not been changed on its way and has not been
received before.

## Rationale

THR-0001, risk value 4, treatment reduce. Handed to the customer for the cybersecurity
concept of the door module (clause 9 is the customer's); the expected control is the
secured communication of the customer's communication stack for the switch request
(IFC-0006).
