## Claim

The risk of THR-0002 can be retained: while the body network is flooded the window does
not move, and that harms nobody.

## Rationale

A window that stays where it is traps nobody (DMG-0003 has no safety impact). The attack
needs a device on the bus inside the cabin or at the mirror connector and ends when it is
removed. The window control does not start any movement of its own while requests are
missing (SWR-0009: manual movement only while the switch is held).
