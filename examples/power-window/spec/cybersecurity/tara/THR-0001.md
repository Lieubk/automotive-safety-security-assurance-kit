## Description

A thief wants the window of a parked, locked vehicle open. A switch request that does
not come from the driver's switch panel, or that was recorded earlier and replayed, is
taken by the window control as a request to open (STRIDE: spoofing).

## Treatment rationale

Risk value 4 is above what the plan allows to retain. Reduce: only authentic and fresh
switch requests may move the window (CSG-0001). The control belongs to the
communication stack of the customer's basic software, so the goal goes to the customer's
cybersecurity concept.
