title: An occupant is trapped by a closing window
status: approved
impact: {safety: severe, financial: negligible, operational: negligible, privacy: negligible}
---

## Description

The window closes on an occupant's neck or arm, typically a child's, and does not
reverse, because the anti-pinch protection does not act.

## Impact rationale

Safety severe: the functional safety lead derived it from the severity of the hazard
"trapping by the closing window" of the customer's hazard analysis. Financial,
operational, privacy negligible: beyond the injury nobody loses money, a function or
data.
