## Description

The window of a parked, locked vehicle opens without the owner's request, and things
left in the vehicle are stolen.

## Impact rationale

Financial moderate: a loss the owner notices, limited to one vehicle. Safety
negligible: nobody is in the vehicle. Operational and privacy negligible: the vehicle
stays usable, no personal data is involved.
