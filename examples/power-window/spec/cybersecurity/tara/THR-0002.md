## Description

Someone with a device on the body network keeps the bus busy, so that the switch
requests of the driver's switch panel no longer reach the window control (STRIDE:
denial of service).

## Treatment rationale

Risk value 3 is above `retain_up_to` (2), so the retention is decided by the
cybersecurity lead, as `decision` records. Reasons in CLM-0001. Protecting the bus as a
whole is the customer's.
