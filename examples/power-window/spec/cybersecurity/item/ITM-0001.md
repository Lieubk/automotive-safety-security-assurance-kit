## Boundary and functions

The item is the window control of the front door module: the window control software
(SYE-0001) and the window drive hardware (SYE-0002) with its motor and Hall sensor.
Outside the item: the driver's switch panel and the other door modules, the body
network, the central gateway, the diagnostic tester, the basic software of the door
module. Functions: opening and closing on a switch request, automatic closing by one
touch, reversal after an obstacle.

## Preliminary architecture

Window control software (SYE-0001) and window drive hardware (SYE-0002), connected by
the Hall sensor pulse signal (SYI-0001) and the motor command to the bridge driver
(SYI-0002). Switch requests reach the software over the body network (SYI-0003) through
the communication stack of the basic software.

## Operational environment

The body network links the door modules, the switch panel and the central gateway. The
gateway connects it to the diagnostic port inside the cabin and to the telematics unit.
Bus wiring runs through the door hinge and to the mirror connector. Workshops reprogram
the door module through the diagnostic port; the bootloader is part of the basic
software. Vehicles are parked unattended.

## Assumptions

The communication stack of the basic software can protect the authenticity and
freshness of signals on the body network. The bootloader accepts only signed software
images.
