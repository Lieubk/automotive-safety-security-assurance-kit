## Scope

The components of ASIL B - WindowPos, AntiPinch, MotorDrive - and the interfaces of the
basic software they use, measured against the system requirements of ASIL B the customer
allocated to the software (SYR-0001, SYR-0002). The basic software itself is analysed by
the customer.

## Method

For each output of each component, the guide words no value, wrong value, too late, too
early and stuck, followed to the movement of the window.

## Results

Four findings, all closed: three carried by software requirements and the execution model,
one left with the customer and argued. The analysis assumes the timing figures of the
execution model, which only the customer's measurement on the target confirms.
