## Scope

A problem is every deviation from an approved item that is found after its approval:
a failed test case whose cause is not the test itself, a finding of a review on an item
that was not the subject of that review, and every report of the customer or of the
team of the window lifter platform. Everyone in the project may report one; the
customer and the platform team receive the alerts this plan names.

Problems in the customer's basic software and in the door module are reported to the
customer and not resolved here. Problems found by the customer on the vehicle reach
the project as a report of the customer and are recorded like any other.

## Approach

Every problem is a `problem-report` in `records/problems/`, moved through the states of
the schema:

1. **open** - recorded by whoever found it, with what happens, how to reproduce it and
   where it was found; a provisional severity.
2. **analysed** - the Software lead, or the developer the lead names, finds the cause,
   the origin and the impact (other items, other users of the same component); the
   Software lead confirms the severity.
3. **in-resolution** - the route is chosen: a correction through the authoring skill of
   the item and a review; a change request once the item is in a baseline that is not
   internal; for a component of the window lifter platform, a report to the platform
   team and the corrected release taken over.
4. **resolved** - the correction is approved, the change request implemented, or the
   corrected release integrated.
5. **closed** - accepted by the Software lead, with the run in which the exposing test
   case passes or the review of the correction as evidence.

A problem that is not one, or a duplicate, is rejected with the reason.

Urgent problems (`urgent_severities`): the Software lead decides within one day on an
immediate action - stop of deliveries, a containment in the software, or none with the
reason - and it is recorded in the report.

Alerts (`alerts`): critical problems go to the customer and the Software lead within one
day; major problems to the Software lead and, when a component of the platform is
affected, to the platform team within two days. The alert is recorded as a
communication record about the problem.

Trends: with every project status report, `traceability.py problems` is read for
origin, detection and age; the Software lead writes the reading and the actions as a
problem trend report.

## Criteria

- critical: safety relevant, security relevant, or a delivery has to be stopped;
- major: a software requirement is not met;
- minor: everything else.

Closure: the exposing test case passed in a final run on a build that contains the
correction, or a final review of the corrected item; every change request that resolves
the problem is closed.

## Environment and tools

Problem reports are items under version control; `python tools/traceability.py problems`
lists them with their age, `history <ID>` gives the states over the commits.
`python tools/record.py communication` records alerts, `problem-trends` the trend report.
