## Scope

The window control software (SYE-0001, the components of CMP-0001) at ASIL B, as the
customer's system requirements allocate it. In scope here, as `scope.safety` of
`project.yaml` says: parts 2, 6, 8 and 9 of ISO 26262. The item, its hazard analysis,
the safety goals, the system and the vehicle are the customer's.

Impact analysis: WindowPos and AntiPinch are new. WindowCmd and MotorDrive are taken over
unchanged from platform release 3.2.0, which was developed under ISO 26262; what changes
is their environment - the body network request of this door module and the anti-pinch
reversal - which the safety analyses of this plan cover. The basic software is the
customer's and enters only through its interfaces.

## Approach

Software safety requirements carry the ASIL of the system requirements they are derived
from. Every software component of ASIL B is analysed with an FMEA at software level;
WindowCmd (QM) coexists with ASIL B components, so a dependent failures analysis argues
freedom from interference. The methods per table of parts 6 and 8 are the ones
`method_selection` names, carried by the strategies listed there. Tools that build or
analyse the delivered code are evaluated.

## Criteria

Every analysis finding is carried by a requirement or design element, or argued. The
confirmation reviews pass before the release candidate.

## Environment and tools

The items in this repository; `tools/traceability.py safety`; the tools evaluated in
TEV-0001 and TEV-0002.

## Responsibilities

Safety manager: this plan, the tool evaluations, the safety case. Software architect:
the safety analyses. A safety engineer of another project: the confirmation reviews.
Customer: everything at system and vehicle level, the target and production.
