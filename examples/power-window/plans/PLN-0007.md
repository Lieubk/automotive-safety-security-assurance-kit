## Scope

Quality assurance of release R1.0: work products of the software requirements, the unit
design and the qualification tests, and the processes of unit verification,
integration and qualification. The other processes are checked by the reviews of their
items and by the process gate the team runs; their results reach quality assurance
through `practices` and the quality report.

## Approach

Before the release candidate baseline, the quality engineer carries out both checks of
`quality_checks`:

- **Work product sample**: per type in `scope` the item with the highest ASIL and the item
  changed last. Checked: the approving review (checklist applied at its version, a result
  for every check, reviewers who did not write the item) and the content against the
  checklist of the type. A model may prepare a reading against the checklist; it is input,
  the verdict is the quality engineer's.
- **Process audit**: per process in `scope`, its strategy against its records - test
  runs, builds, reviews - and against `practices`; the process gate report of the team
  is read as input, not repeated.

Every confirmed finding gets an action before it is recorded: a corrective action in
`records/actions/` with owner and due date, or a problem report when a work product is
wrong (PLN-0006). The effect of an action is shown in the next check or report before it
is closed.

Escalation (`escalation`): an action overdue by more than 7 days goes to the project lead
in the weekly project meeting, by more than 21 days to the head of development by mail.
The answer is recorded with the escalation.

Reporting: a quality report after each check round, before every baseline whose purpose
a check names, to the project lead and the software lead.

## Criteria

A work product conforms when its approving review is complete and independent and the
sample shows no deviation from the checklist; a process conforms when its records show
what its strategy promises. A deviation either way is a finding.

## Environment and tools

`python tools/traceability.py quality`, `practices`, `check`, `show`; the process gate
report; `python tools/record.py quality-check`, `quality-report` and `communication`.
