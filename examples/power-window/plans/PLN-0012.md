## Scope

The development of the window control software for the customer's front door module, as
ordered for release R1.0. The customer is responsible for the item and the vehicle; the
project for the software at ASIL B.

## Approach

Safety-relevant information is exchanged with each delivery baseline and when a party
finds a problem that concerns the other: problem reports within five working days,
changes of safety requirements as new versions of the system requirements.

## Criteria

The agreement holds while its activities match the safety plan (PLN-0010) and the
customer's safety plan.

## Environment and tools

Exchange of items and records as files of the delivery baseline.

## Responsibilities

Safety managers of both sides; the customer's contact for the software is named in the
order.
