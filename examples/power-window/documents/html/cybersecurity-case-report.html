Argument

Demonstration data: written for the worked example, not by a person who stands behind it.

The window control software is cybersecure enough for its part of the door module. The threat analysis on the customer's item definition (ITM-0001) found three threat scenarios against the two assets the software holds: the switch request from the body network and the software image. None was left without a decision:

  • THR-0001 (forged open request, risk 4) is reduced by CSG-0001; the control is the secured communication of the customer's communication stack, and the customer took the goal into its cybersecurity concept at the hand-over (CMR-0004).
  • THR-0002 (bus flooding, risk 3) is retained by the decision of the cybersecurity lead, argued in CLM-0001: a window that does not move harms nobody.
  • THR-0003 (manipulated image, risk 4) is shared with the customer, whose bootloader accepts only signed images (CLM-0002), as the customer confirmed (CMR-0004).

Every risk value is the value of the risk matrix of PLN-0008, and the analysis, the goal and the claims are reviewed (REV-0010, REV-0011). Not covered by this case: the cybersecurity concept, validation and post-development, which are the customer's (clauses 9 and 11 to 14), and an independent assessment, which the plan does not foresee.
