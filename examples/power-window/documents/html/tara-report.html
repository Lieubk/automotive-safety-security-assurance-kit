Item definition

ITM-0001 Window control of the front door module

Received from Front Door Module - Item Definition for Cybersecurity, version 1.0, identifier ID-FDM-WIN.

statusapproved

Boundary and functions

The item is the window control of the front door module: the window control software (SYE-0001) and the window drive hardware (SYE-0002) with its motor and Hall sensor. Outside the item: the driver's switch panel and the other door modules, the body network, the central gateway, the diagnostic tester, the basic software of the door module. Functions: opening and closing on a switch request, automatic closing by one touch, reversal after an obstacle.

Preliminary architecture

Window control software (SYE-0001) and window drive hardware (SYE-0002), connected by the Hall sensor pulse signal (SYI-0001) and the motor command to the bridge driver (SYI-0002). Switch requests reach the software over the body network (SYI-0003) through the communication stack of the basic software.

Operational environment

The body network links the door modules, the switch panel and the central gateway. The gateway connects it to the diagnostic port inside the cabin and to the telematics unit. Bus wiring runs through the door hinge and to the mirror connector. Workshops reprogram the door module through the diagnostic port; the bootloader is part of the basic software. Vehicles are parked unattended.

Assumptions

The communication stack of the basic software can protect the authenticity and freshness of signals on the body network. The bootloader accepts only signed software images.

THR-0001 Forged open request while the vehicle is parked

statusapproved
propertyauthenticity
feasibilityhigh
risk_value4
treatmentreduce

attack_paths

device clipped to the bus wiring at the mirror connector from outside the locked vehicle -> forged switch request 'open' -> WindowCmd opens the windowmediumvector: adjacent; note: the mirror connector is reached without opening the vehicle

Description

A thief wants the window of a parked, locked vehicle open. A switch request that does not come from the driver's switch panel, or that was recorded earlier and replayed, is taken by the window control as a request to open (STRIDE: spoofing).

Treatment rationale

Risk value 4 is above what the plan allows to retain. Reduce: only authentic and fresh switch requests may move the window (CSG-0001). The control belongs to the communication stack of the customer's basic software, so the goal goes to the customer's cybersecurity concept.

Cybersecurity goals

CSG-0001 Only authentic and fresh switch requests move the window

statusapproved

Statement

The window shall move on a request received over the body network only if the request comes from the driver's switch panel, has not been changed on its way and has not been received before.

Rationale

THR-0001, risk value 4, treatment reduce. Handed to the customer for the cybersecurity concept of the door module (clause 9 is the customer's); the expected control is the secured communication of the customer's communication stack for the switch request (IFC-0006).

Cybersecurity claims

CLM-0001 Loss of window movement during a bus flooding attack is acceptable

statusapproved
monitored_byreported to the customer with the TARA (CMR-0004); bus attacks are watched by the customer's vehicle security monitoring

Claim

The risk of THR-0002 can be retained: while the body network is flooded the window does not move, and that harms nobody.

Rationale

A window that stays where it is traps nobody (DMG-0003 has no safety impact). The attack needs a device on the bus inside the cabin or at the mirror connector and ends when it is removed. The window control does not start any movement of its own while requests are missing (SWR-0009: manual movement only while the switch is held).
