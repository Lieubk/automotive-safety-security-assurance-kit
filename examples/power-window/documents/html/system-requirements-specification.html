SYR-0001 Limitation of the closing force

Received from Front Door Module - System Requirements Specification, version 2.1, identifier DM-SYS-041.

Statement

While the window closes with a window opening of more than 5 mm, the door module shall keep the force on an obstacle in the path of the glass at or below 80 N.

Verification criteria

A test body with a stiffness of 10 N/mm is placed on the upper edge of the glass at window openings of 10 mm, 150 mm and 400 mm, at supply voltages of 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V. The peak force in each of the nine runs is at most 80 N.

SYR-0002 Reversal after an obstacle

Statement

When an obstacle has been detected while the window closes, the door module shall open the window by 120 mm from the position of detection, or to the fully open position if less than 120 mm of travel remain.

Verification criteria

With the test body of DM-SYS-041 at window openings of 10 mm and 150 mm the glass comes to rest 120 mm +/- 5 mm further open than at the moment of contact. At 400 mm it comes to rest fully open (480 mm).

SYR-0003 Automatic closing by one touch

Received from Front Door Module - System Requirements Specification, version 2.1, identifier DM-SYS-017.

Statement

When the switch request AUTO_CLOSE is received, the door module shall close the window completely without the request being held, unless a later switch request or a detected obstacle ends the movement.

Verification criteria

AUTO_CLOSE applied for 100 ms at window openings of 480 mm and 200 mm: the window reaches the fully closed position. OPEN applied during the movement: the closing movement ends within 100 ms.

SYR-0004 Manual movement while the switch is held

Received from Front Door Module - System Requirements Specification, version 2.1, identifier DM-SYS-016.

Statement

While the switch request is OPEN or CLOSE, the door module shall move the window in the requested direction, starting and ending the movement within 100 ms after the request has changed.

Verification criteria

OPEN and CLOSE each applied for 1 s at a window opening of 240 mm: the motor is driven in the requested direction from at most 100 ms after the start of the request until at most 100 ms after its end.
