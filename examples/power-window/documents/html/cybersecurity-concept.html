Cybersecurity goals

CSG-0001 Only authentic and fresh switch requests move the window

statusapproved

Statement

The window shall move on a request received over the body network only if the request comes from the driver's switch panel, has not been changed on its way and has not been received before.

Rationale

THR-0001, risk value 4, treatment reduce. Handed to the customer for the cybersecurity concept of the door module (clause 9 is the customer's); the expected control is the secured communication of the customer's communication stack for the switch request (IFC-0006).

Cybersecurity claims

CLM-0001 Loss of window movement during a bus flooding attack is acceptable

statusapproved
monitored_byreported to the customer with the TARA (CMR-0004); bus attacks are watched by the customer's vehicle security monitoring

Claim

The risk of THR-0002 can be retained: while the body network is flooded the window does not move, and that harms nobody.

Rationale

A window that stays where it is traps nobody (DMG-0003 has no safety impact). The attack needs a device on the bus inside the cabin or at the mirror connector and ends when it is removed. The window control does not start any movement of its own while requests are missing (SWR-0009: manual movement only while the switch is held).

CLM-0002 The customer's bootloader keeps manipulated software images out

statusapproved
monitored_bythe customer's cybersecurity monitoring of its basic software, as agreed at the TARA hand-over (CMR-0004)

Claim

The risk of THR-0003 is carried by the customer: only software images signed by the customer can be written into the door module.

Rationale

The item definition (ITM-0001) states that the bootloader of the basic software accepts only signed images and that reprogramming needs its security access. The customer confirmed both at the TARA hand-over and takes the risk into its own analysis (CMR-0004).
