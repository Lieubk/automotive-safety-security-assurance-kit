## Description

Cause: the unit adds status and command messages to a body CAN shared with other units.
Event: the bus load of the body CAN exceeds what the OEM allows. Consequence: messages of
the unit are delayed and the OEM asks for rework of the message set.

## Analysis

Probability 1 (unlikely) and impact 2 (rework of one element) are the owner's rating.
Exposure 2 is at the acceptable level of the plan (2), so no treatment is needed.

## Treatment and monitoring

No treatment: the risk is accepted. The bus load is read again when the OEM delivers its
message matrix.
