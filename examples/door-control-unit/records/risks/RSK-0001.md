## Description

Cause: the motor supplier builds the B-samples for several customers and the date of
2026-10-20 is its own estimate. Event: the ten B-sample motors arrive after 2026-10-20.
Consequence: integration step 2 on the door bench cannot start and release B1 on
2026-11-19 is missed.

## Analysis

Probability 2 (possible): the supplier named 2026-10-20 without a dated commitment; this is
judgement of the owner, no earlier delivery of this supplier is on record in this project.
Impact 3: a milestone missed. Exposure 6, above the acceptable level of the plan.

## Treatment and monitoring

Weekly check of the delivery status with the supplier, owner L. Leader. The indicator is
the delay of the delivery in days; at 5 days the owner decides a corrective action. The
delivery arrived on 2026-10-20 (see the supplier records), the delay was 0 days and the
risk is treated.
