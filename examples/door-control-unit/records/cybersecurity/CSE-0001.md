## Argument

The cybersecurity case of the door control unit DCU-F for release B1, for the clauses 6 and 8 that the project is responsible for.

Why the project's cybersecurity management is adequate for a B-sample: the cybersecurity plan PLN-0005 decides every subclause of clauses 6 and 8; the subclauses 6.4.4 to 6.4.6 and 6.4.8 are not applicable, each with its reason. The monitoring plan PLN-0006 names the sources and triggers, and it was used: the bulletin of 2026-11-05 was received, triaged as relevant and led to the weakness WKN-0001, which was analysed for exploitability and rated.

What is accepted: the weak seed-key algorithm of the bootloader stays in B1. Its feasibility is low because reprogramming needs the diagnostic port and the OEM's tester credentials; the OEM is told (CMR-0003) and the corrected bootloader goes into B2. The weakness is not closed: it stays in resolution until B2, and the release note of B1 lists it as a known issue. Clause 15 and the item's TARA are the OEM's and are not part of this case. No independent cybersecurity assessment was agreed.
