## Description

The bootloader of the basic software supplier in the software element SYE-0002 derives the key of the programming session from a seed with a weak algorithm (bulletin of 2026-11-05, CEV-0001). The bootloader version is the one flashed on the B-samples of release B1.

## Analysis

Exploitability: reprogramming needs the vehicle diagnostic port and the OEM's tester credentials; the feasibility by attack vector is low. The impact on the OEM's assets is not known to the project, because the OEM's TARA has not been delivered: for the rating the highest impact of the matrix, severe, is assumed, which with low feasibility gives the risk value 4 of the matrix in PLN-0005. The OEM is told as information sharing (CMR-0003) and may rate the impact itself.

Treatment: retain. The corrected bootloader of the basic software supplier goes into B2; until then the risk is retained by K. Cyber (CLM-0001).
