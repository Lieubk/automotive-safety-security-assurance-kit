## Minutes

Technical review with the motor supplier, 2026-10-27. Participants: S. Motor (supplier), E. Engineer.

Reviewed: the test report of the delivery of 2026-10-20 (10 B-sample motors) against the interface SYI-0004 and SYI-0003 of the project. Speed and torque of the motors agree with the values the project needs for the closing time. The test report shows the Hall signal at room temperature and at 85 C with a duty cycle of 50 percent +/- 5 percent. At -40 C the report shows a duty cycle outside 50 percent +/- 10 percent in 3 of 10 motors. Problem and risk: the evaluation of the Hall signal at -40 C depends on it. Decision: the supplier analyses the cause and reports; the project keeps the interface definition of SYI-0004 unchanged until then.
