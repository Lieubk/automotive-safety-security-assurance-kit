# Front door control unit - requirements

Document DCU-F-RQ, version 1.0, 2026-09-15. OEM door systems. Invented for an example.

## DCU-F-1 One-touch window movement
A short press of the window switch beyond the second detent shall move the window fully
up or fully down without further action of the occupant.

## DCU-F-2 Anti-pinch
During automatic closing the window shall reverse when an obstacle opposes it with a force
of more than 100 N, and shall never exert more than 100 N on it.

## DCU-F-3 Central locking
A lock or unlock command received from the body controller on the body CAN shall be
executed by the door lock within 300 ms.

## DCU-F-4 Diagnostics
The fault memory of the door control unit shall be readable and erasable through the
vehicle diagnostic interface.

## DCU-F-5 Environment
The door control unit shall operate at supply voltages from 9 V to 16 V and at
temperatures from -40 C to 85 C.
