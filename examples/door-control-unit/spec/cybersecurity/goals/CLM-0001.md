## Claim

The risk of WKN-0001 is retained in release B1: the weak seed-key algorithm of the bootloader stays in the B-samples until release B2.

## Rationale

Exploiting it needs access to the vehicle diagnostic port and the OEM's tester credentials, so the attack feasibility is low and the risk value 4 is reached only under the assumption of the highest impact. B1 is a B-sample release that the OEM integrates for tests; the bootloader of the basic software supplier is corrected in a later release that goes into B2. The retention is decided by K. Cyber, responsible for cybersecurity, accepted for the project by L. Leader as project lead, and is known to the OEM contact (CMR-0003). The release note of B1 lists the weakness as a known issue.
