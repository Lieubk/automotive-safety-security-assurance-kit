## Context

The closing force limit (SYR-0004) and the reversal at an obstacle (SYR-0003) need a measure of the force on the glass. The motor current is proportional to the motor torque and so to the force. The current can be sensed in the electronics of the door ECU or in the motor. The choice fixes which element carries the sensor and which interface carries the value.

## Evaluation criteria

1. **Reaction time**: the current value reaches the software within 1 ms of the change, so that the reversal fits the 140 ms chain of VIEW-0002. Weight 3.
2. **Motor as supplied**: the B-sample motor of the motor supplier stays unchanged, because the samples are already ordered (RSK-0001). Weight 3.
3. **Cost per unit** of the added sensing. Weight 2.
4. **Interfaces**: no further signal wire between motor and unit. Weight 1.

## Alternatives

**A - Sensing in the door ECU hardware.** A shunt in the H-bridge and an amplifier give the current to the converter of the microcontroller.

**B - Sensing in the motor.** The motor carries a current sensor and sends the value on an extra signal line to the unit.

| Criterion | A - in the hardware | B - in the motor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reaction time | converter sampled every 1 ms - fulfilled | depends on the sensor electronics of the motor, not known - not fulfilled as far as known |
| 2 Motor as supplied | motor unchanged - fulfilled | motor has to be changed and the B-samples ordered again - not fulfilled |
| 3 Cost | shunt and amplifier on the board, low - fulfilled | sensor in every motor, higher - not fulfilled |
| 4 Interfaces | no extra wire - fulfilled | one extra signal line in SYI-0003 or SYI-0004 - not fulfilled |

## Decision

Alternative A, sensing in the door ECU hardware. B fails the criteria 2 and 1, which carry the delivery date and the reversal time. Decided by L. Leader, project lead, on 2026-10-13, on the proposal of E. Engineer.

## Consequences

- The door ECU hardware (SYE-0001) carries the shunt, the amplifier and the converter channel, and delivers the current value every 1 ms to the software element.
- The motor (SYE-0003) needs no change; the interface SYI-0003 carries no extra signal.
- The hardware department confirms the converter channel in the schematic of the B-sample hardware.
