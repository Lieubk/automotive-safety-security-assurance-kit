## Responsibility

The DC motor of the window lifter with the gear and the Hall sensor on the motor shaft, supplied by the motor supplier as B-samples with a test report for each delivery. The project uses the motor as delivered; the drawing is the supplier's, drawing changes are communicated to us.

For the system it delivers the speed that, with the speed ramp of the software, closes the window from fully open to fully closed within 4.0 s (SYR-0002), and the Hall pulses that give the position. The temperature range of -40 C to 85 C holds at the housing of the unit, not at the motor (deviation of STK-0005).

Not part of this element: driving the motor (SYE-0001), the evaluation of the Hall signal (SYE-0002).

## Dynamic behavior

- The Hall signal delivers no pulses while the motor stands.
- The duty cycle of the Hall signal at -40 C is an open item with the motor supplier (technical review of 2026-10-27).
