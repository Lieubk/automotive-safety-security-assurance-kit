## Responsibility

The electronics of the door control unit, developed by our hardware department: microcontroller, power supply, H-bridge for the window lift motor, current sensing in the bridge, lock driver, body CAN transceiver, LIN transceiver and the capture input for the Hall signals. It offers the software the motor current as a measured value, the Hall edges as captured times and the bus frames.

For the system it holds the supply range of 9 V to 16 V (SYR-0009) and the housing temperature range of -40 C to 85 C (SYR-0010). It senses the motor current in the bridge (decision ADR-0001) and delivers it to the software element, so that the closing force limit of SYR-0004 and the reversal of SYR-0003 can be fulfilled by the software.

Not part of this element: the evaluation of the current and the reversal logic (SYE-0002), the motor itself (SYE-0003).

## Dynamic behavior

- Start-up: the supply is stable and the microcontroller is released from reset within 20 ms after the supply exceeds 9 V.
- State after reset: the bridge output is off and the lock driver does not move.
- Current sensing delivers a new value every 1 ms.
