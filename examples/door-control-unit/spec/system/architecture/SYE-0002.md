## Responsibility

The software of the door control unit, supplied by our software department as the released element "power window software 1.0" and integrated as received. Its inside is described by that department's software architecture and is not part of this system architecture.

It evaluates the switch states from the LIN frames, moves the window to its end position, limits the closing force by the measured motor current, reverses at an obstacle, executes lock and unlock commands of the body CAN and serves reading and erasing of the fault memory on the diagnostic interface. For SYR-0002 it shares the closing time with the motor: the software sets the speed ramp, the motor delivers the speed. For SYR-0001 it shares with the switch panel: the panel delivers the detent states, the software moves the window.

Not part of this element: the measurement of the motor current and the Hall capture (SYE-0001), the motor (SYE-0003).

## Dynamic behavior

- Cycle times: 5 ms for position, current evaluation and motor command; 20 ms for the evaluation of the switch frame.
- Reaction to an obstacle: the changed motor command at most 40 ms after the force exceeds 100 N at the obstacle (see VIEW-0002).
- Start-up: ready 50 ms after reset is released, window stopped, position not known until the first end stop.
