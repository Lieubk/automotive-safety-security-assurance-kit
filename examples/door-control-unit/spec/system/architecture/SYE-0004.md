## Responsibility

The window switch panel in the door, supplied by the OEM and not designed in this project. It delivers the switch states, including the passing of the second detent, to the door control unit as LIN frames. What is assumed of it: it sends the state of each switch every 20 ms and a pressed switch beyond the second detent in the frame within 40 ms.

Not part of this element: the movement of the window (SYE-0002).

## Dynamic behavior

The panel sends its frame every 20 ms from its own start-up; a missing frame for 100 ms is treated as no switch pressed by the door ECU software.
