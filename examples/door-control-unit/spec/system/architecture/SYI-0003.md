## Definition

End points: the H-bridge of the door ECU hardware (SYE-0001, provider) and the window lift motor (SYE-0003). The bridge applies the supply voltage to the motor terminals with pulse width modulation.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Voltage at the terminals | 0 V to the supply voltage, 9 V to 16 V at the supply, tolerance +/- 0.5 V at 13.5 V |
| Pulse width modulation | 20 kHz, duty cycle 0 percent to 100 percent in steps of 1 percent |
| Current | up to 12 A continuous, peak 25 A for at most 100 ms |
| Direction | open, close or off; off connects both terminals to ground |
| Protection | the bridge switches itself off at an over-temperature of 150 C or a current above 30 A |

## Dynamic behavior

A new command takes effect at the terminals within 1 ms.
