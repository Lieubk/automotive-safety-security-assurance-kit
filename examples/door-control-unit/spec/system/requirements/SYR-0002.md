## Statement

When the window is closed by a press of the window switch beyond the second detent, the door control unit shall close the window from fully open to fully closed within 4.0 s.

## Verification criteria

At a supply voltage of 13.5 V and a temperature of 23 C, with the window fully open, the switch is pressed beyond the second detent in closing direction. In each of five runs the time from the press to the fully closed position is at most 4.0 s.

## Analysis

The source states no time. The limit of 4.0 s is the project's measurable reading of "fully up without further action"; it is not confirmed with the OEM and depends on the speed ramp of the motor, which is calibratable. Feasibility is open until the first bench measurement.
