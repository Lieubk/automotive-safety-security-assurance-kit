## Statement

When the window switch is pressed beyond the second detent, the door control unit shall move the window in the direction of the switch to its end position without further action of the occupant.

## Verification criteria

With the window at mid-stroke, the switch is pressed beyond the second detent once in each direction at a supply voltage of 13.5 V and a temperature of 23 C. After the press and without further action the window reaches the fully open and the fully closed position respectively; the end position is observed on the bench's window position sensor.

## Analysis

The switch panel sends the detent state to the unit; the unit has to detect the end position without a limit switch, which asks the motor to report its position. Neighbours: the door switch panel (OEM) provides the switch states, the motor with its Hall sensor provides the position.
