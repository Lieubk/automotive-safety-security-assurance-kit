## Statement

During automatic closing the door control unit shall limit the force that the window exerts on an obstacle to at most 100 N.

## Verification criteria

A test body with a stiffness of 10 N/mm is placed in the path of the glass at window openings of 150 mm and 400 mm. The peak force at the test body is measured in closing runs at 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V and at housing temperatures of -40 C, 23 C and 85 C. In each run the peak force is at most 100 N.

## Analysis

The force depends on the stiffness of the obstacle and on the reversal time of SYR-0003; both are set by the test body. The force sensor of the bench is needed.
