## Statement

The door control unit shall operate at housing temperatures from -40 C to 85 C.

## Verification criteria

In the temperature chamber of the bench the housing of the unit is held at -40 C, 23 C and 85 C. At each temperature and at 13.5 V the unit executes a window movement of SYR-0001 and a lock command of SYR-0005 within their limits.

## Analysis

The range holds at the housing, not at the motor: the deviation of STK-0005 agreed with O. Engineer on 2026-09-22 (CMR-0001). The Hall signal duty cycle of the motor at -40 C is an open item with the motor supplier.
