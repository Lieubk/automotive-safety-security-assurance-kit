## Statement

The door control unit shall operate at supply voltages from 9 V to 16 V.

## Verification criteria

At supply voltages of 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V and a temperature of 23 C, the unit executes a window movement of SYR-0001 and a lock command of SYR-0005 within their limits.

## Analysis

The motor current at 9 V is the worst case for the closing time of SYR-0002. The power supply of the vehicle has to hold 9 V during reversal.
