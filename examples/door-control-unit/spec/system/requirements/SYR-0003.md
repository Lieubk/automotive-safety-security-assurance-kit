## Statement

During automatic closing, when an obstacle opposes the window with a force of more than 100 N, the door control unit shall reverse the movement of the window.

## Verification criteria

A test body with a stiffness of 10 N/mm is placed in the path of the glass at window openings of 150 mm and 400 mm. The window closes automatically at 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V. In each of the six runs the window changes direction and moves downwards by at least 50 mm within 200 ms after the force reaches 100 N.

## Analysis

The source states no reversal time or distance. The 50 mm and 200 ms are observation limits chosen for the bench test; they are not requirements of the OEM and are to be confirmed with the OEM. The detection needs the motor current, which asks the system to sense it (architecture decision).
