## Statement

When the door control unit receives a lock or unlock command, it shall have executed the command at the door lock within 300 ms after the end of the message on the body CAN.

## Verification criteria

A lock command and an unlock command are sent on the body CAN by the bench's bus simulation, ten times each, at 13.5 V and 23 C. In each run the door lock has reached the commanded position within 300 ms after the end of the message; the time is measured between the bus message and the lock position signal.

## Analysis

The lock actuator is part of the door and is driven by the unit; its own travel time is part of the 300 ms. The body controller (OEM) has to send the command on the body CAN as defined in SYR-0006.
