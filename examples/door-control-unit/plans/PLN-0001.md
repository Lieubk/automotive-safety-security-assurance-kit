## Scope

Goal: the front door control unit DCU-F (electronics, software and the window lift motor
as one unit) for the OEM's vehicle line, as B-sample release B1, from the OEM's
requirements "Front door control unit - requirements" version 1.0 (see `inputs`).
Delivered: the unit samples, the system requirements and architecture, the system
integration and qualification test evidence, the release note and the baselines. Not part
of it: the software of the unit, which comes as a released element from our software
department and is integrated as received (its SWE processes are theirs); integration of
the unit into the vehicle and the vehicle-level cybersecurity work, which are the OEM's.
Processes in scope are those of `project.yaml`: SYS.2 to SYS.5, ACQ.4, SUP.8, SPL.2,
MAN.3, MAN.5.

## Approach

One release, B1, in three phases - system requirements, system architecture, integration
and qualification on the bench - each ended by a milestone. Changes after a baseline go
through change requests. Effort is estimated in person-days per work package by the owner
with the project lead, from the size of the deliverables.

## Criteria

The milestone "release candidate" is reached when a baseline with that purpose is
recorded and communicated. The milestones "System requirements agreed" and "System
architecture frozen" have no baseline of their own: the first is reached when the
review record of the system requirements approves them, the second when the review
record of the system elements, interfaces and views approves them. The plan is revised
when a milestone is at risk by more than one reporting period, when the scope changes,
or when a resource is lost.

## Environment and tools

Git repository, the toolkit's trace engine and record tool. The door bench of the test
department for integration and qualification runs, with its JUnit reports; ten B-sample
motors from the motor supplier; B-sample hardware of the door ECU from our hardware
department. The versions of the bench and its software are named in each run record.

## Feasibility

Facts: three milestones over 9 weeks, two system engineers, the bench of the test
department, ten motor samples due 2026-10-20. Assumptions: the OEM keeps its requirements
version 1.0 stable; the motor samples arrive on 2026-10-20. Risks: the motor samples may be
late (R1), the EMC emission of the motor bridge may need rework (R3). Decision: feasible
with two engineers and the bench of the test department, decided by L. Leader on
2026-09-24.

## Reporting

A status report every month to the OEM contact and to management, written with
`/project-status`: progress against the work packages, milestones, risks and open
corrective actions. A corrective action is decided by the project lead when a work
package is late at a reporting date or a milestone is at risk.
