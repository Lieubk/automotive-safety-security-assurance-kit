## Scope

The parts of the unit that come from outside and are watched: the bootloader and the basic software supplied by the basic software supplier, with the versions named in the manifests of the system builds (BLD-0001 to BLD-0003), and the OEM's diagnostic and body CAN interfaces SYI-0001. The window lift motor has no software and is not watched for weaknesses. The OEM's TARA is not part of this project.

## Approach

K. Cyber collects the sources at their rhythm and triages each piece against the triggers: it is an event when a trigger applies to a part and version of the unit. Every event is recorded, also one that is not relevant. A relevant event becomes one weakness per weakness shown, analysed within 10 working days for exploitability by attack vector and rated by the risk matrix of the cybersecurity plan. A vulnerability is treated by reduce (a corrected release of the part, carried by the supplier's release), share (told to the OEM) or retain (with a claim). The OEM contact is told of every relevant event as information sharing.

## Criteria

A triage is on time when it is done within 5 working days of receipt. An event is closed when its weaknesses exist or the reason for not relevant is recorded. A weakness is closed when the person who accepts it states that the treatment works, with the evidence.

## Environment and tools

The items of this repository and `traceability.py check` and `clauses`; the sources above.

## Responsibilities

K. Cyber triages, analyses and proposes the treatment; L. Leader accepts the retention of a risk and the closure of a weakness; E. Engineer supports the analysis from the system architecture.
