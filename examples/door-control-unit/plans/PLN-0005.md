## Scope

The door control unit DCU-F as supplied to the OEM, for the clauses 6 (project dependent cybersecurity management) and 8 (continual cybersecurity activities) of ISO/SAE 21434. Left to the OEM: the item definition, the TARA, the cybersecurity goals and concept, validation and everything in the vehicle (clauses 9 to 11 and 15). The OEM's TARA has not been delivered to this project; the rating method above is the OEM's as the project applies it to a vulnerability, and a rating of the OEM that differs replaces it.

## Approach

K. Cyber is responsible for cybersecurity in the project and for every activity of the plan except the release. Monitoring, triage, analysis and treatment of weaknesses follow the monitoring plan (PLN-0006) and are recorded as events and weaknesses. A weakness is analysed for exploitability with attack paths rated by attack vector, and rated with the risk matrix above. A risk is retained only with a claim; the OEM is told of every relevant event as information, because the impact on its assets is its to judge. No cybersecurity assessment is agreed (subclause 6.4.8); the plan is revised if the OEM asks for one. The cybersecurity case for the release is prepared by K. Cyber from the work products of the clauses in scope, and the release is approved by L. Leader.

## Criteria

The plan is complete when every subclause of the clauses 6 and 8 has a decision, and `check` reports no COV-53. The work is on time when every relevant event has a weakness within 5 working days and every vulnerability a treatment. The plan is revised when the scope, the responsibilities or the method change, and before each release.

## Environment and tools

The items of this repository, `traceability.py clauses` and `traceability.py check`; the bulletins of the basic software supplier and the advisories of the OEM as the monitoring plan names them.

## Responsibilities

K. Cyber: the plan, the monitoring, the analysis and the treatment decisions of weaknesses, the cybersecurity case. L. Leader: the release for post-development and the resources of the plan. E. Engineer: supports the analysis of attack paths from the system architecture.
