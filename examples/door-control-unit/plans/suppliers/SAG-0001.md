## Joint processes

The supplier builds and tests the motors to its own processes; the project does not assess them. The two sides meet in a technical review every 4 weeks and in a progress review every month. Changes to the drawing go from the supplier to the project before they apply; the project decides in its own change handling whether a changed drawing is accepted. A defect found in a sample is reported by the project to the supplier and handled in the project's problem resolution. A delivery is accepted by the project when the samples are complete and the test report is received. The supplier was selected by the purchasing department before the project; no supplier evaluation is part of this project.

## Interfaces and responsibilities

The motor (SYE-0003) meets the door ECU hardware in two interfaces. The motor power SYI-0003 and the Hall signals SYI-0004 are defined by the project; the supplier delivers a motor that works within them. The project verifies the interfaces on the bench in the system integration test, the supplier verifies the motor in its test report. Drawing and sensor data of the motor are the supplier's; the project may not change them.

## Deviations and escalation

A deviation is a delivery that is late by more than one week, a delivery without a test report, or an open item of a review that is not closed by its due date. The project lead is told by the system engineer, and decides what is done. A deviation that stays open after a further progress review is taken by the project lead to the supplier's contact. A changed objective is written into this agreement and reviewed again.
