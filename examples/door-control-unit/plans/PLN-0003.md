## Scope

Integrated here: the door ECU hardware (SYE-0001) with the software element (SYE-0002) in the first step, the window lift motor (SYE-0003) and the door switch panel (SYE-0004) in the second. The software element is the released element of our software department, integrated as received; its build is named by its version in the manifest of each system build, and its inside is not tested here. The switch panel is the OEM's external element: it takes part as the OEM's sample, and is simulated in the first step.

Not integrated here: the body controller and the vehicle, simulated on the body CAN by the rest-bus simulation in every step; the integration into the vehicle is the OEM's. Left to the OEM: behaviour in the vehicle.

## Approach

Two steps along the signal path: first the software on the electronics, with the motor and the panel simulated, so that the interfaces SYI-0001 to SYI-0004 are tested without a motor; then the real motor and panel on the door bench. Per step the cases verify the system interfaces and the behaviour in VIEW-0002: each signal reaches its receiver, arrives in time and in the stated range, and the elements behave together over time. Cases are derived from the definitions of the interfaces and from VIEW-0002, never from a sample.

Regression: a change of an element or of the software element makes the steps that contain it stale and they are integrated and run again. A change of the calibration of the software element that leaves its interfaces unchanged is integrated and run again in the last step only (the step "door-bench"); the step "software-on-hardware" is repeated when the interfaces of the software element change.

## Criteria

A step starts when the samples and the version of the software element are identified in its manifest and the earlier step passed. A step passes when every case of the step has the verdict passed in the latest run on the latest build of the step; this is read from `check` (COV-18, COV-49) and the run record. A failed case gets a problem report or a decision of the project lead; no case is skipped to pass a step.

## Environment and tools

Bench "door-bench-electronics": B-sample door ECU on the bench supply, Hall emulator, LIN and body CAN simulation of the test department. Bench "door-bench": the same with a B-sample motor in the door frame and the OEM's switch panel. Each bench reports its results as JUnit XML with the test case ID in the name of each test; its test system release and setup are named in the manifest of the build. The bench cannot show behaviour in the vehicle or in the climate chamber.

## Responsibilities

The system engineer (E. Engineer) derives the cases and assembles the steps with the bench team. The bench test lead (V. Verifier) runs the bench and reviews the cases; the cases are reviewed by someone other than their author. The project lead (L. Leader) decides on a failed case.
