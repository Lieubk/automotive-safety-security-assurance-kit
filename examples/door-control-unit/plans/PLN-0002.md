## Scope

The risks of the DCU-F project to release B1: technical, schedule and supplier risks.
Commercial and vehicle-level risks are the OEM's and the organization's. No
organizational risk policy applies; the scales and the threshold below are the project's.

## Approach

Risks are looked for at the project plan, at each design decision, change request and
problem report, at each supplier review and at each monthly status meeting. A risk is
written in state `identified` with cause, event and consequence, rated by its owner on
the scales below (`analysed`), then either treated (`in-treatment`, with owned actions and
indicators) or accepted (`accepted`). A risk above the acceptable level is accepted only
by a named person with a recorded decision. The register is read by exposure with
`traceability.py risks` at every reporting date, and the owners state the indicator
values. Treatment counts as not progressing when an action is overdue or an indicator
reaches its threshold; the project lead then decides a corrective action. The risk
figures go into each status report; a risk with exposure 6 or more is told to the OEM
contact at the next status meeting.

## Criteria

Exposure is probability times impact. Probability and impact have the meanings in the
attributes of this plan. Up to exposure 2 a risk needs no treatment: a delay of under
ten days at most possible is absorbed by the schedule buffer before B1. Above 2 a risk is
treated or accepted by the project lead with a recorded reason.

## Environment and tools

Risks as items in `records/risks/`; `traceability.py risks`; the risk figures in the
project status report.

## Responsibilities

The project lead (L. Leader) accepts risks above the acceptable level, decides corrective
actions and reports risks to the OEM contact. Risk owners rate their risks, carry out or
assign the treatment and state the indicator values at each reporting date. Every team
member may identify a risk.
