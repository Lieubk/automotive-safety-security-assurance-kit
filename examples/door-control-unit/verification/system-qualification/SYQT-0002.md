## Preconditions

Complete system on the door bench, window fully open, supply 13.5 V, temperature 23 C; stop watch on the bench's window position signal.

## Steps

1. Press the closing switch beyond the second detent; measure the time from the press to the fully closed position; repeat until five runs are done, opening the window in between.

## Expected result

1. In each of the five runs the time from the press to the fully closed position is at most 4.0 s.
