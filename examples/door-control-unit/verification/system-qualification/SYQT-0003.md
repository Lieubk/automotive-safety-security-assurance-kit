## Preconditions

Complete system on the door bench; test body of 10 N/mm in the path of the glass; window openings of 150 mm and 400 mm; supply voltages 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V, six runs.

## Steps

1. Close the window automatically against the test body.
2. Measure the force at the test body, the window direction and the displacement after the force reaches 100 N.

## Expected result

1. In each of the six runs the window changes direction and moves down by at least 50 mm within 200 ms after the force reaches 100 N.
