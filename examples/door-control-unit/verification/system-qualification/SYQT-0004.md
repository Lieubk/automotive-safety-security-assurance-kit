## Preconditions

Complete system on the door bench in the temperature chamber; test body of 10 N/mm; window openings 150 mm and 400 mm; supply voltages 9 V, 13.5 V and 16 V; housing temperatures -40 C, 23 C and 85 C.

## Steps

1. Close the window automatically against the test body in each combination of opening, voltage and temperature.
2. Record the peak force at the test body.

## Expected result

1. The peak force is at most 100 N in every run.
