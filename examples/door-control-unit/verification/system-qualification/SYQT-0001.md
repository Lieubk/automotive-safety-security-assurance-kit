## Preconditions

Complete system on the door bench, window at mid-stroke, supply 13.5 V, temperature 23 C.

## Steps

1. Press the closing switch beyond the second detent and release it.
2. Move the window to mid-stroke again, press the opening switch beyond the second detent and release it.

## Expected result

1. The window reaches the fully closed position without further action.
2. The window reaches the fully open position without further action.
