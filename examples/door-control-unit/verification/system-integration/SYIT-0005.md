Basis: SYI-0003 (Definition: direction open, close or off, current up to 12 A continuous, peak 25 A for at most 100 ms).

## Preconditions

Step door-bench on the bench door-bench; B-sample motor on the window in the door frame, window fully open; supply 13.5 V; current probe on the motor line.

## Steps

1. The software commands closing with a duty cycle of 100 percent for 1 s.
2. The software commands opening with a duty cycle of 100 percent for 1 s.
3. The software commands off and the bench waits 200 ms.

## Expected result

1. The window moves upwards; the motor current stays at or below 12 A continuous and below 25 A peak for at most 100 ms.
2. The window moves downwards; the current limits are the same.
3. The window stands still within 200 ms.
