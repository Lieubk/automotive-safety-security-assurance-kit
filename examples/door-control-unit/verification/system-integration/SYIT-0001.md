Basis: SYI-0001 (Definition: LockCmd 1 lock and 2 unlock, LockAck within 20 ms, checksum protection).

## Preconditions

Step software-on-hardware on the bench door-bench-electronics; the rest-bus simulation of the body controller sends on the body CAN at 500 kbit/s; the unit is in normal operation, no fault stored, supply 13.5 V.

## Steps

1. The rest-bus simulation sends LockCmd 1 with a valid checksum.
2. The rest-bus simulation sends LockCmd 2 with a valid checksum.
3. The rest-bus simulation sends LockCmd 1 with a wrong checksum.

## Expected result

1. LockAck 1 is sent within 20 ms and the lock driver output changes to the lock direction.
2. LockAck 2 is sent within 20 ms and the lock driver output changes to the unlock direction.
3. No LockAck is sent and the lock driver output does not change.
