Basis: SYI-0004 (Definition: 0 to 400 Hz, one edge per 0.5 mm of glass travel, a standing motor delivers no edges).

## Preconditions

Step software-on-hardware on the bench door-bench-electronics; the Hall emulator on the capture input, two channels with 90 degrees phase shift, 5 V; supply 13.5 V; position read through the diagnostic read-out.

## Steps

1. The Hall emulator delivers 400 Hz on both channels for 1 s in closing direction.
2. The Hall emulator stops delivering edges and the bench waits 200 ms.

## Expected result

1. The position value changes by 200 mm +/- 1 mm.
2. The software reports the window as standing.
