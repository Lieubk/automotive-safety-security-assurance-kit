Basis: SYI-0004 (Definition: one edge per 0.5 mm of glass travel, duty cycle 50 percent +/- 10 percent, 5 V logic level).

## Preconditions

Step door-bench on the bench door-bench; B-sample motor on the window, window fully open; supply 13.5 V; oscilloscope on both Hall channels; position measured by the bench's reference sensor.

## Steps

1. The software moves the window upwards by 100 mm measured at the reference sensor.
2. The bench measures the high level and the duty cycle of the Hall signal during the movement.

## Expected result

1. The software counts 200 edges +/- 2 and reads a position change of 100 mm +/- 1 mm.
2. The high level is 5 V +/- 0.5 V and the duty cycle is 50 percent +/- 10 percent.
