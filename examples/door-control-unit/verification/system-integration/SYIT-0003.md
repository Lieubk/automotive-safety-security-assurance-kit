Basis: SYI-0003 (Definition: pulse width modulation 20 kHz, duty cycle 0 to 100 percent in steps of 1 percent, direction open, close or off, a new command takes effect within 1 ms).

## Preconditions

Step software-on-hardware on the bench door-bench-electronics; the load emulation of the motor, 12 V, 8 A, connected to the terminals; supply 13.5 V; oscilloscope on both terminals.

## Steps

1. The software commands closing with a duty cycle of 50 percent.
2. The software commands opening with a duty cycle of 100 percent.
3. The software commands off.

## Expected result

1. The terminals show a pulse width modulation of 20 kHz with a duty cycle of 50 percent, in closing polarity, within 1 ms of the command.
2. The terminals show the supply voltage 13.5 V +/- 0.5 V in opening polarity within 1 ms of the command.
3. Both terminals are connected to ground within 1 ms of the command.
