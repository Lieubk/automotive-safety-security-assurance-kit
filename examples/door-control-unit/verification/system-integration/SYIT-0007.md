Basis: SYI-0002 (Definition: one frame per 20 ms, state 2 second detent) and SYE-0004 (Dynamic behavior: the panel sends every 20 ms, a press beyond the second detent within 40 ms).

## Preconditions

Step door-bench on the bench door-bench; the OEM's switch panel on the LIN; B-sample motor on the window, window fully open; supply 13.5 V.

## Steps

1. The bench operator presses the closing switch beyond the second detent and the LIN is recorded.
2. The bench observes the window.

## Expected result

1. The frame with state 2 appears within 40 ms of the press, with a valid checksum.
2. The window starts to close within 20 ms after the frame.
