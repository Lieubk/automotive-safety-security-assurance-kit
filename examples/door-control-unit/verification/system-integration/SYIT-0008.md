Basis: VIEW-0002 (Sequence: 5 ms detection, 35 ms command and bridge, 100 ms motor, 140 ms in total from detection to the change of direction).

## Preconditions

Step door-bench on the bench door-bench; B-sample motor on the window, window fully open; test body of 10 N/mm at an opening of 150 mm; supply 13.5 V; force sensor and the motor current recorded.

## Steps

1. The bench operator presses the closing switch beyond the second detent.
2. The window closes until it touches the test body and the force rises above 100 N.
3. The bench measures the time from the motor current at the limit to the change of the motor direction.

## Expected result

1. The window starts to close without further action.
2. The current reaches the limit that corresponds to 100 N.
3. The motor direction changes at most 140 ms after the current limit and the window moves down.
